David Zucca 1963–2006

David Zucca was a Canadian artist whose work blended classical draftsmanship with emotional subtlety and symbolic depth. Trained at the Ontario College of Art and Humber College, he further refined his technique in Florence, Italy, before returning to Canada.

He eventually settled in Stratford, Ontario, where he taught computer design at the high school level while continuing his artistic practice. Over time, his work evolved to explore bolder uses of color and texture, with recurring themes of intimacy, memory, and love woven throughout his paintings.

His pieces—ranging from dreamlike portraits to meditative abstractions—were exhibited and collected across Canada, the U.S., and Europe. Whether painting a stone doorway or a surreal inner vision, Zucca consistently sought stillness, clarity, and emotional resonance.
